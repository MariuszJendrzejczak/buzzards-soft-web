**Whole-page repetition sweep (hero vs. the rest of `mobileOffer.*`):** load-bearing selling
points and their one home — **AI ⇒ faster/cheaper** → `audience.body2` (hero only *names*
"wspierając się AI" once, glancingly); **within a small business's reach / accessible price**
→ `audience.heading`+`body2` (hero does **not** state price or affordability at all);
**publication on the client's own accounts** → `guide.publish` + `includes.publication`
(hero's heading carries "opublikowana" as the promise, detail lives there); **pay once /
everything is yours** → `ownership` (hero's heading names "w całości Twoja" **once** as the
promise→payoff bookend the sweep explicitly permits); **process / 5 steps** → `guide`;
**store bureaucracy / timeline** → `publishing`; **one accountable person** → `audience.body3`
(hero touches it once via "Buduję go sam… bezpośredni kontakt ze mną"). No point is pitched
in the hero that another section then re-argues; `audience.body1`'s verbatim echo of the old
hero is designed out. **Sweep clean.**

**Drift diagnosis:** The single through-line risk this section had to fight was
**promise-inflation into a whole-page summary** — the instinct (visible in the "before"
hero) to prove the entire offer in the first paragraph: published + owned + AI-cheaper +
within-reach + code/project/data, all at once. That instinct is exactly what created the
cross-section echoes (`audience`, `guide`, `includes`, `ownership` each re-arguing a point
the hero had already spent) and the near-verbatim `audience.body1` repeat. The fix was
subtractive, not additive: hold the **Rule of One** at the *page* level, let the **heading**
carry the one primary promise (published + owned) as a bookend, and re-assign the subheading's
three sentences to jobs no other section does (product-for-your-business → light one-expert/AI
note → invitation). A secondary, smaller drift was a **`rozm-` root echo** (s2 "rozmawiasz" →
s3 "rozmowy") introduced while chasing warmth; resolved by swapping s2 to "bezpośredni kontakt
ze mną". No negation-contrast, infantile closer, `na`-for-`dla`, or migration/leave phrasing
appeared in any draft — the owner hard rules held throughout.
