**Sweep clean.** The "two parallel tracks from day one" fact is legitimately shared as a *headline* in `guide.intro` + `guide.publish` and explained in full in `publishing.points.twoTracks` — a promise→payoff bookend (the sweep's allowed exception), not a re-pitch: `guide` states *that* publication starts day one; `publishing` explains *why* (the specific bureaucratic gates). No selling point is argued twice.

**Drift diagnosis:** The one recurring pull this section had to resist was **absorbing neighbouring sections' pitches into the process narrative** — the natural instinct when writing "how it's built" is to reach for the AI-price story (in the build step), the ownership story (in the publish step), and the accountability story (again, in the build step), because each *feels* like it belongs to "how I work". That instinct produced two concrete v1 misses: the build step first duplicated `audience.body3`'s *"każdą decyzję… podejmuję i sprawdzam osobiście"* almost verbatim (a Rule-of-One / dim-8 collision), and the support step reached for a `, a nie zostawiony z nią sam` negation-contrast closer (owner hard-rule violation) that also risked the infantile "helpless client" register. Both were fixed subtractively: scope the build step's human-oversight to *the build itself* (not the whole offer), and close the support step on a concrete adult outcome (*"spokojne, dopilnowane wejście… na rynek"*) instead of a negation. A third, smaller drift was a **pause-dash creep** (two em-dashes across the section, over the ≤1 rule); resolved by rewriting the intro's parenthetical dash into a `bo`-clause. No `na`-for-`dla`, migration/leave phrasing, or infantile peppy send-off survived into the final; the owner hard rules held throughout.
