**Whole-page repetition sweep (pricing's contribution):** pricing states the four
load-bearing facts it owns (prices, scope, screens, timeline) and **references** — never
re-pitches — the two claims other sections own: the baked-in standard (`includes` via
`baseReference`) and running costs / ownership (`ownership` via the footnote pointer).
No selling point that belongs to `audience`/`guide` (AI-price mechanic, one-expert,
publication-as-differentiator) is re-argued in a pricing leaf. Clean.

**Drift diagnosis:** The natural drift for a pricing block is two-fold, and both were
actively resisted. (1) **Repetition creep** — a pricing card wants to re-justify itself
by re-listing the baked-in standard on every tier and by restating the Apple/Google cost
numbers „for clarity"; the discipline was to keep exactly one pointer each
(`baseReference`, footnote) and let `includes` / `ownership` own the detail, so the Rule
of One holds across the page rather than only within this section. (2) **Negation reflex
around Full-vs-Start and platform fees** — the tempting frame is „Start nie ma kont" /
„opłaty platform nie są wliczone"; both were rewritten affirmatively (`„Full dokłada:"` /
`„Start + konta…"` and „opłacasz bezpośrednio u dostawców") so no leaf defines a tier or
the price by a lack. The only remaining dashes are numeric-range łączniki, which are
correct PL punctuation, not the em-dash AI-tell.

## Open Questions

1. **Should the mobile offer carry a dated promo (e.g. the web's „do końca sierpnia"),
   or price flat under „cena startowa" with no end date?** — `mobile-offer-facts.md`
   marks the promo window **⚠ CONFIRM** (Open item #2). This copy deliberately asserts a
   start-of-offer framing with **no date** (`promoNote` = „cena startowa"; `footnote`
   = „obowiązuje w okresie startu oferty"). If the owner wants a hard deadline, both
   `promoNote` and `footnote` need a dated variant and this becomes a time-boxed claim
   the site must honour. *Why it matters:* a dated promo is a claim-precision (dim 11)
   commitment — asserting a date we don't intend to enforce would be a credibility
   defect; asserting none keeps the copy honest until the owner decides.

2. **Are 9 900 / 19 900 (net) final, or still working figures?** — the facts sheet marks
   tier prices **⚠ CONFIRM** (Open item #1; „accepted as working figures 2026-07-20").
   The copy states them as fact. *Why it matters:* prices are the load-bearing claim of
   this section; if they move, the copy regenerates from the sheet, not the reverse.

3. **Is store publication a bundled inclusion in every tier (assumed here), or an explicit
   add-on?** — facts sheet Open item #3. This affects `includes`, not a pricing leaf
   directly, but the pricing `scope` lines assume publication is baked in (so it's not
   priced separately in any tier). *Why it matters:* if publication becomes an add-on, a
   pricing row or footnote line would need to name it.
