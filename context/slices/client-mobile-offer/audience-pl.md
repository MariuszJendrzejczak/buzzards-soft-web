**Drift diagnosis:** The through-line risk this section carried was **claim-poaching disguised as
warmth** — the "before" `body2` reached for the hero's most reassuring words (*"prawdziwy produkt na
własność"*) to make the affordability point land emotionally, and in doing so re-pitched two claims
that live elsewhere (hero's published-owned promise, `ownership`'s "na własność"), leaving audience's
*actual* job — explaining *why* the price is low — underdeveloped and blurred into a generic reassurance.
The fix was to trust the claim ledger: strip every borrowed claim, and let `body2` do the one thing only
audience can do — give the buyer a *credible reason why* an AI-augmented build costs less (fewer hours,
priced by time, same careful work), which is a stronger trust move for a skeptical local buyer than
repeating "it's a real product". A secondary, smaller drift was a **`prac-` root adjacency** in body2
(*"przyspiesza pracę"* → *"czas pracy"* one sentence apart) and a latent negation reflex around
*"nie prototyp"*; both were resolved affirmatively (lead with *"produkt gotowy do pracy"*, break the
`prac-` adjacency by reopening s2 on *"godzin… na zbudowanie aplikacji"*). No `na`-for-`dla`, no
infantile closer, no migration/leave phrasing appeared in any draft — the owner hard rules held throughout.
