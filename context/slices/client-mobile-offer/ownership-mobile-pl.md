**Whole-page claim-ledger check (cross-section, Rule of One):** ownership is the sole home
for pay-once + everything-is-yours + the three named running costs + the three honest
limits. It does **not** restate: the AI/economics pitch (`audience.body2` + `guide`), the
prices (`pricing`), store bureaucracy / D-U-N-S / 12-tester gate (`publishing`), the backend
**feature** or store-publication **feature** (`includes` — ownership names only the *running
cost* of the backend), or the 30-day support **feature** (`guide.support` + `includes` —
ownership references it only as the maintenance boundary). `pricing.footnote` already points
here ("opisane w sekcji „Wszystko jest Twoje”"), so the costs live in exactly one place. No
selling point is re-pitched.

**Drift diagnosis:** The current copy's defects clustered around three reflexes the
standard names explicitly — (a) a **negation-first** payoff (*"nie ma abonamentu u mnie"*)
and a **negation-first limits heading** (*"Czego nie robię…"*), both of which make the
reader hold the absent thing in mind; (b) **pause-dash slogans** hung off headings
(*"— uczciwie"*, *"— i mówię to od razu"*), a strong PL AI-tell and a pitch signal; and (c)
one **colloquialism** (*"działka"*) below the register floor. The rewrite holds all the
load-bearing content (ownership, the three real costs, the three honest limits) unchanged in
substance and re-frames each as an affirmative statement of what the client *gets* or what a
boundary *is*, so nothing reads as defensive or salesy. Ownership is affirmed and closed
without any leave/portability angle, per the owner hard rule.

## Open Questions

1. **Graphics route wording — "recommended designer" vs no referral.** The mobile facts
   sheet lists *"a recommended designer"* as an honest graphics route, but on the **web**
   offer the owner cut exactly that referral promise ("nie chcę się w to bawić", dropped
   *"polecę Ci sprawdzonego specjalistę"*). I softened it to *"grafik, z którym
   współpracujesz"* (no referral promise). Confirm whether mobile should (a) keep no
   referral like web, or (b) actively point to a recommended designer — this changes
   `limits.graphics.body`. *Why it matters:* a referral you don't want to stand behind is a
   support commitment; the web decision suggests "no referral" is the owner's preference.
2. **Maintenance plan beyond 30 days — sell one, or stay build-only?** (Facts sheet Open
   Q4.) The copy currently states maintenance is *"osobne ustalenie… wyceniam według
   zakresu"* and deliberately omits the 10–20%/yr rule-of-thumb. If the owner decides to
   sell a named maintenance plan, `limits.maintenance.body` should surface its shape (and
   this limit may move out of "honest limits"). *Why it matters:* it changes whether this is
   framed as a boundary or as an upsell.
3. **Running-cost currency — leave Apple/Google in USD, or add a PLN hint?** Facts sheet
   gives Apple/Google in USD (their billing currency) and backend in zł. Copy keeps them as
   the platforms bill them. Confirm no PLN conversion is wanted (FX drift would date any
   fixed złoty figure). *Why it matters:* a stale PLN figure is a Claim-precision (dim 11)
   risk; the USD figures are stable and correct.
