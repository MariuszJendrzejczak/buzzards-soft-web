## Brief recap & the decision this mirrors

The **web** sibling (`messages/pl.json → offer.quote`) was deliberately reworked by the
owner so the flow is: **contact form → I reply to arrange a short call / online meeting
→ the quote comes only after that unhurried talk.** The **current mobile** quote reverts
this — both its heading (*"…odezwę się z wyceną"*) and intro (*"Wrócę z propozycją
zakresu i wyceną"*) push an **immediate wycena**. This rewrite fixes that: it leads with
the invitation to write, then arranging a conversation, and **defers the wycena to after
the talk**, matching the web decision.

**Claim ledger (this section owns / must NOT restate):**
- **Owns:** the one primary CTA of the page — invite the reader to write, arrange a
  call/meeting, quote after. `ctaPrimary "Wyceń swoją aplikację"` (hero + pricing) points
  here.
- **Does NOT restate:** prices (owned by `pricing`), ownership / pay-once (owned by
  `ownership` + `includes`), the process steps (owned by `guide`). The "everything starts
  with a conversation" idea is *named* in `hero.subheading` and `guide.step.conversation`;
  here it becomes the **payoff** (the actual invitation to have that conversation) — a
  legitimate promise→payoff bookend, not an echo.

**Drift diagnosis:** The single risk this section had to clear was the one the brief
named — the pull toward promising an **immediate wycena** in the heading/intro (the
current copy's defect, and the natural reflex for a "quote" beat). The fix held the Rule
of One to *one* job — get the reader to write and open a conversation — and pushed the
price to a single deferred, calm mention (*"już po tej rozmowie"*), mirroring the web
decision rather than re-deriving it. No negation-contrast reflex fired (sweep clean), no
price/ownership/process re-pitch leaked in from neighbouring sections, and the register
stayed peer-to-peer without sliding into either the *"call"* anglicism (ban-list §G2 →
used *"rozmowę telefoniczną"*) or an infantile pat-closer (ban-list §G1 → ends on the
concrete adult outcome *"Wycenę dostaniesz spokojnie, już po tej rozmowie"*, not *"i
gotowe"*). Converged without needing a regeneration pass: the mirrored web exemplar gave
the paragraph-role discipline directly.

## Open Questions

1. **Promo/wycena timing language** — the facts oracle marks the promo window
   (*do końca sierpnia* mechanic) as ⚠ CONFIRM for mobile and the tier prices as working
   figures. This copy deliberately says **nothing** about price amount or a promo
   deadline (it defers the whole wycena to the post-call talk), so it is safe either way
   — but if the owner later wants the CTA to carry any urgency ("promo do końca
   sierpnia"), that is a fact that must be confirmed in `mobile-offer-facts.md` first and
   would change this intro. Banked, not invented.
2. **"rozmowa telefoniczna albo spotkanie online"** mirrors the web quote verbatim in
   intent. Confirm the owner still wants **both** modes offered for the mobile audience
   (vs. e.g. video-call-only). Not a blocker — the web decision is the default carried
   here.
