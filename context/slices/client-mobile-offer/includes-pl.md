**Sweep clean.** No selling point that `includes` owns is re-argued in another section, and
`includes` does not poach any point another section owns — it only *names* the platforms,
backend, publication, and ownership where an adjacent section explains or bounds them.

**Drift diagnosis:** The single through-line risk here was **lane-bleed under the pressure to
add weight** — the very fix the owner asked for (turn thin one-liners into meaty
feature→benefit blocks) tempts each item to reach for the most persuasive nearby fact, which
for this section is almost always a fact another section owns: the running costs (`ownership`),
the AI-price story (`audience`), the store-timeline honesty (`publishing`), or the prices
(`pricing`). Two concrete drifts surfaced and were fixed subtractively. First, an early
`items.backend` draft ended *"a nie zamknięte u kogoś innego"* — a **double violation**: a
negation frame (owner zero-tolerance) *and* a vendor-lock-in/migration hint (the owner hard
rule §L), so it was cut, leaving the affirmative *"Dane… należą do Ciebie i są pod Twoją
kontrolą"*. Second, `items.ownership` initially drifted toward the running-cost payoff
(*"bez abonamentu"*, a hint at platform fees) — pulled back to a clean one-line inclusion
(*"na własność… Płacisz raz"*) so the full ownership+costs argument stays in `ownership`. The
governing discipline was **Rule of One at the page level, not the item level**: give each item
real weight, but only about the thing it uniquely delivers. No infantile pat-closer,
`na`-for-`dla` slip, or migration/leave phrasing survived into the final draft — the owner hard
rules held throughout.

## Open Questions

*(Facts `includes` deliberately does **not** assert — banked here rather than guessed. None
block the copy above.)*

1. **Is "firmowych kont" the right default in `items.accounts`, or should it stay account-type
   neutral?** — The facts sheet marks the individual-vs-organization store-account default as
   ⚠ CONFIRM (`mobile-offer-facts.md` §Open/to-confirm #6), because it drives the D-U-N-S
   critical path. This copy says *"firmowych kont"* (company accounts) to match a small-business
   audience, but if the default should also cover a **private** Google Play account (which
   triggers the 12-tester closed-test gate), consider neutral wording like *"kont w sklepach"*.
   *Why it matters:* the account type the reader assumes here sets expectations that `publishing`
   then details; a mismatch reads as inconsistent. The timeline/gate detail itself correctly
   stays in `publishing`, not here.

2. **Does `items.theme` overclaim by pairing "wygląda dobrze o każdej porze" with the
   dark/light fact?** — The dark/light + PL/EN inclusion is confirmed (`mobile-offer-facts.md`
   §"What's included"). The added *"wygląda dobrze o każdej porze"* is a benefit gloss, not a
   new fact — flagged only so the owner can confirm the framing reads as *elevation included*
   (the facts-sheet guardrail) and not as fixing a deficient base. *Why it matters:* the
   guardrail is explicit in the facts sheet; the phrasing here is intended to satisfy it, but
   it is a judgment call worth a human glance.

3. **Should `items.support` name what "wsparcie" covers within the 30 days?** — The facts sheet
   confirms *30-day post-launch support* but does not enumerate its scope (bug-fixes only? small
   tweaks?). This copy keeps it deliberately unscoped (*"reaguję na bieżąco"*) rather than
   invent a scope. *Why it matters:* if the owner wants the boundary explicit (e.g. "poprawki
   błędów" vs new features), that is a fact to add to the sheet first, then to the copy — and it
   partly overlaps the `ownership.limits.maintenance` line, so it should be decided across both
   sections at once.
